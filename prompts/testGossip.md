I just ran into Old Meritt by the mill, muttering to himself again. He said he saw a lantern flicker out by the abandoned orchard last night, even though nobody’s lived there since the fire. He swears he heard someone humming too, a tune he couldn’t place but said it made his skin crawl. He told me not to go near the place after sunset. Strange thing is, when he walked away, I noticed soot on his sleeves. Fresh. I don’t know what he was doing out there, but he definitely didn’t want to talk about it.
